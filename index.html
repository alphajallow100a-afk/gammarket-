❓ Help Center - All Rules

1. How to Deposit:
Wallet → Tap "Deposit D1000" (test). Real: Wave / Bank. Balance increases. Use to pay monthly fee and to buy products via wallet transfer.

2. How to Withdraw - 1% Fee:
Wallet → Enter amount in Withdraw box → Tap Withdraw. Min D100. Fee = 1% of amount. For every D100 you withdraw, fee is D1. Example: Withdraw D100 → Fee D1 → You receive D99. Withdraw D1000 → Fee D10 → You receive D990. Fee is taken from your wallet balance.

3. How to Transfer Money to Merchant:
Wallet → Transfer Money to Merchant section → Enter merchant phone, amount, product note → Tap "Transfer to Merchant - Escrow Hold" → Money moves from your wallet to escrow.

4. Escrow - Money Stays Until Received:
After transfer/buy, money stays in Escrow Held. Seller must deliver. Buyer goes to Orders → Taps "✅ Tap as Received" → 100% to merchant wallet. GamMarket takes 0% from escrow.

5. How to Pay Monthly Subscription:
First 2 months: D300 per month. After first 2 months: D1000 per month, every month. Overdue >30 days → Sell blocked. Go Wallet → Pay Due Now. You must pay to keep selling.

6. How GamMarket Earns:
GamMarket earns ONLY from: (a) Monthly subscription - D300 for first 2 months, then D1000/month, and (b) Withdrawal fee - 1% of every withdrawal. GamMarket does NOT take commission from escrow sales.

7. Sell & Profile Pic:
Sell: Upload product photo auto-compress 600px → Lat/Lng → Post. Profile: Upload round circle 300px compressed.

8. Map & Chat:
Map pins show merchant locations. Tap pin → View profile & Chat.

9. Verification:
You must verify with ID card to sell. No one is auto-verified. Everyone must verify, including admin.

Contact GamMarket Support in the app for help.
